How these fit together

The Setup Guide gets you legally operating. Cargo Securement is about what happens after that — the rules an officer applies at a scale, on a load you have already been paid to move. They do not overlap, and neither one repeats the other.

Neither is a filing service and neither is legal advice. Both exist so you can do this yourself, from the regulations, instead of paying someone a percentage of your business for the same information.
