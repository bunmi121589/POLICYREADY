What this is, plainly

This is a guide, not a filing service and not legal advice. It explains what each federal requirement is, why it exists, what it costs, and exactly who to contact — so you can do it yourself instead of paying someone a percentage to do it for you.

Where a government form already exists, we tell you where to get it rather than selling you a copy of it. Where a dollar figure or phone number appears, it came from an official source, and we tell you to confirm anything that can change before you rely on it.

It does not cover your state's own registration and tax rules. Those come next.
