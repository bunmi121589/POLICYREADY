What this is, and what it is not

This is the federal cargo securement rules written out in plain language, with every working load limit table reproduced and every commodity rule explained. It is not a filing service, it is not legal advice, and it does not certify anything. It exists so you can do this yourself, correctly, instead of finding out what the rule was from an officer at a scale.

Every figure comes from the regulation itself, and every rule is cited so you can read it free at ecfr.gov. If anyone tells you something that contradicts what you read there, the regulation wins.
